The Campus Reality

Why coordinating university schedules is exhausting

Every department runs on different bells. Staggered start times and irregular lab sessions leave college friends disconnected.

01

The 50-Minute Asymmetry

One friend has classes from 08:30 to 09:20. Another starts at 09:00. Standard calendars force rigid 1-hour blocks that falsely claim people are free when they're sitting in lecture halls.

02

Group Chat Coordination Fatigue

“Who’s free after 2 PM?” “I have physics lab until 4.” “Can we do 4:30?” “I have tutorial.” Ten messages back and forth just to discover a 30-minute window for coffee or project prep.

03

Lost Spontaneous Serendipity

The best university moments happen in the unscheduled 45-minute gaps between lectures. Without visibility, you sit in the library while your closest friends are sitting alone across campus.

Core Technology

The Continuous Interval Matching Engine

We solved the hard problem: calculating mathematical intersections across non-standard schedules.

Minute-by-Minute Overlap

Instead of matching rigid slot strings like "09:30 - 10:20", UniSync converts every timetable entry into minutes from midnight. Classes overlap only when mathematically confirmed.

Disjoint Gap Inversion

We merge all squad members' busy intervals, sort them, and invert them against university active hours. The remaining gaps are 100% guaranteed free time for everyone.

Duration Thresholds

Filter out negligible 5-minute passing breaks. Choose minimum hangout duration (at least 30 minutes, 45 minutes, or 1 hour) so every suggested slot is actionable.
