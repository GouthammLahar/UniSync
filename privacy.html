Public Disclosure

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026  •  App: UniSync (unisynclive.vercel.app)

1. Introduction

Welcome to UniSync. We build student timetable coordination and campus productivity tools designed to help university peers discover common free periods, organize group study sessions, and coordinate campus meetings seamlessly.

Your privacy and control over your academic data are fundamental to our architecture. This Privacy Policy explains what information UniSync collects, how it is stored and protected, how it is shared within authorized squads, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Account Information Collected

When you sign up or log in to UniSync, we collect essential profile information via Google Sign-In:

  • Full Name and Display Name: Used to identify you to squad members and meeting participants.
  • Email Address: Used for account identity, authentication, squad membership verification, and meeting invitation delivery.
  • Profile Photo (Avatar): Displayed in squad presence lists, meeting response cards, and chat headers.
  • Firebase User Identifier (UID): A unique system-generated identifier used to securely associate your timetables, squad memberships, and settings.

3. Timetable & Schedule Availability

UniSync enables students to import timetables (via Excel spreadsheet upload or manual period entry) to calculate free and busy intervals.

  • Timetable Details: Course names, day-of-week slots, start and end times, and optional room locations.
  • Storage & Squad Isolation: Timetable data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore protected by strict security rules. Your schedule is private and is only accessible to authenticated members of campus squads that you explicitly join using an invite code.
  • No Public Discovery: Your class schedule is never made public on the web, indexed by search engines, or made visible to students outside your squads.

4. Google Sign-In & Authentication

Authentication is powered by Google Identity Services and Firebase Authentication. When signing in:

  • We request only standard OpenID profile and email scopes.
  • We do not receive, store, or have access to your Google account password.
  • Sessions are maintained via secure, short-lived tokens verified on every backend request.

5. Optional Gmail Connection Feature

Important: Connecting your Gmail account is entirely optional. It is requested strictly when you choose to connect Gmail, and is not required to sign in, use timetables, find free slots, or join squads.

UniSync provides an optional meeting email capability powered by Google OAuth 2.0 with the following minimal permission scope:

  • Requested Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send
  • Sole Purpose: To send meeting invitation, status update, confirmation, cancellation, and reminder emails directly from your own Gmail account on your behalf when you schedule or manage a meeting.
  • Recipient Delivery: Meeting emails are addressed exclusively to the registered email addresses of participants you select for that meeting.
  • Zero Inbox Access: UniSync does not read, view, parse, index, search, or delete your emails, contacts, or mailbox contents. The gmail.send scope grants outbound delivery capability only.
  • Server-Side Token Security: Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely in restricted server-side databases with administrative access control. They are never exposed to client-side code or third parties.
  • One-Click Disconnect & Revocation: You can disconnect your Gmail at any time from the Meetings interface. Disconnecting immediately removes your stored credentials and revokes the active token with Google.
  • No Fallback Sending: If you have not connected Gmail, UniSync will not send emails on your behalf or impersonate another sender.

6. Meeting Details & Notifications

When scheduling meetings in UniSync, we record the meeting title, description, selected time, location, participant UIDs, and participant responses (accepted, declined, or pending).

  • In-App Notifications: Saved in Firestore and shown in your real-time notification bell.
  • Push Notifications: Delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to devices where you have granted browser notification permission.

7. Data Security, Retention & Deletion

We implement industry-standard software safeguards to keep student information secure:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption for all client-to-server and API communications.
  • Firestore database access control rules enforcing that only verified group members can query group schedules.
  • Zero sale of personal data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade student data, timetables, or email addresses with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Data Retention & Control: You can leave any squad, delete your uploaded timetable, or cancel meetings at any time directly through the interface.
  • Account Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your UniSync account, profile, uploaded schedules, and meeting history, contact us at the address below.

8. Independent Student Project Notice

UniSync is an independent student-focused campus coordination web application. We do not claim external security certifications (such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2) or statutory compliance seals that have not been formally audited. We commit to transparency, minimal data collection, and robust technical access controls.
